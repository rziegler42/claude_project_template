## CAD work

For CAD modelling, scripting, mesh reverse engineering, image-to-CAD
modelling, assemblies, or manufacturing exports, follow `docs/cad.md`. Where
`docs/cad.md` is more specific than user-level tool guidance, the project
convention wins over that guidance's defaults.

- Report measured results (deviation, volume, bounding box, degrees of freedom),
  not only that a feature recomputed.
